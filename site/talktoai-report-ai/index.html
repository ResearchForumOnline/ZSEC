ZERO ONE and OpenZero support

Report concerning AI output.

Use the public support email to report output that appears unsafe, abusive, misleading, privacy-invasive, or seriously incorrect. This page does not submit a report by itself and does not operate a ticketing system.

Email an AI output report

No report is sent until you review and send the email to shaf@talktoai.org.

Useful evidence

  • Whether this happened in ZERO ONE, OpenZero, or another TalkToAI surface.
  • Date, time, and timezone, plus the app and model version if visible.
  • A short description of what happened and what you expected instead.
  • The smallest safe prompt and output excerpt needed to understand the problem.
  • A redacted screenshot when wording or layout matters.

Do not include secrets

  • Passwords, API tokens, session cookies, private keys, or recovery codes.
  • Confidential documents, complete chat histories, or data you cannot lawfully share.
  • Unnecessary personal data, full local file paths, or unredacted account details.
  • Live malware samples or executable attachments.
Send only evidence you are authorized to share. Your email provider handles delivery of the message. Read the privacy policy.
